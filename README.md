# six-pendulum-cartpole-rl

Training code and experiment log for a cartpole with up to six free-swinging hinged links. The goal was to learn a swing-up from the hanging rest pose and hold the chain upright for at least one second. This repository is the extracted training code from the AI Lab section of https://maxpetrusenko.com. The interactive page is at https://maxpetrusenko.com/ailab/six-pendulum-cartpole.

Short version: reinforcement-learning-style training solved the one-link problem only. Links two through six are not solved by any learned policy here. The six-link solve shown on the live page is a model-based controller from a third-party repository, not learned.

## Results

| Links | Method | Result | Counts as learned solve |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Evolutionary policy search (24 open-loop force knots plus a small MLP feedback term), vectorized analytic simulator, curriculum over gravity and damping | Validation max hold 5.715 s (P10 4.0 s), solved-one-second rate 1.0, 8 s horizon, 229 s on one NVIDIA L4 | Yes, in its own simulator |
| 1 | Same policy evaluated in the MuJoCo Warp environment | Max hold 0.0 s | No (does not transfer) |
| 1 | MuJoCo PPO, SAC, TD3 (Modal, L4) | Best down-start hold under 0.03 s | No |
| 1 | Device-resident recurrent PPO on MuJoCo Warp, with BC, DAgger, curriculum starts, reward variants | Hold-start (starting near upright) solved; exact down-start hold stayed 0.0 s deterministic, best stochastic 0.8 s | No |
| 1 | Same lane, Modal snapshot sequence DAgger | Deterministic exact-down max hold 0.22 s, stochastic 1.49 s on 3/4 passes | No |
| 2 | Hold-first curriculum, CEM, validation-aware search | Best transient held fraction 0.058 of the horizon; strict score 0.0 | No |
| 3 to 4 | Device PPO diagnostic runs only | Sub-second holds, saturated actions | No |
| 6 | m1el/inverted-pendulum: direct collocation plus time-varying LQR on verified N-link dynamics | Nominal and perturbed-start verification pass | No. Model-based, not RL |

Notes on how to read the table.

- The scoring gate is strict. A run only counts if all active links are near upright, the chain is nearly straight, and the pose is held for at least one consecutive second from the exact hanging start. Sub-second flashes are logged as diagnostics and never promoted.
- The one-link evolutionary solve is a black-box policy search, not gradient-based RL. It was trained and validated inside a custom vectorized simulator. When the same policy was run in the MuJoCo Warp environment it held for 0.0 s, so the result is specific to that simulator's dynamics.
- Hold-start results (starting near the top) are stabilization only and do not count toward the swing-up goal.
- The six-link solve comes from https://github.com/m1el/inverted-pendulum, reproduced locally. It uses no learning and is shown on the live page as a separate, labeled result. Nothing in this repository learns a six-link controller.

## Environment

- Cart on a rail with 1 to 6 passive hinged links, generated by `scripts/generate-six-pendulum-mjcf.py` into `app/ailab/six-pendulum-cartpole/mjcf/`.
- Gravity 9.81 (the one-link curriculum starts at 2.2), low or no hinge damping at the final stage, cart tracking toward zero.
- One-link observation: cart x, cart velocity, sin and cos of the link angle, angular velocity, previous action, normalized time. Multi-link environments use 3 + 5 x links values.
- Single continuous action: cart force, scaled by `forceScale`.
- Two simulators: a custom vectorized analytic simulator (fast, used for the one-link solve) and MuJoCo / MuJoCo Warp (used for PPO, SAC, TD3, and the PufferLib-shaped lane).

## Algorithms tried

- Evolution strategies and CEM with time-knot feedback policies (`modal-train-six-pendulum-cem.py`, `-curriculum.py`).
- Pezzza-style vectorized evolution for one link and a chain variant for two links (`-pezzza-evolution.py`, `-pezzza-chain.py`).
- PPO and recurrent PPO (`modal-train-six-pendulum-mujoco-ppo.py`, `train_six_pendulum_mjwarp_device_ppo.py`, `train_six_pendulum_mjwarp_local_ppo.py`), including a MinGRU-style policy with 1,071,107 parameters.
- SAC and TD3 with stabilize-then-swing curricula (`modal-train-six-pendulum-mujoco-sac.py`).
- Behavior cloning and DAgger from a classical energy-pumping teacher, trajectory banks, elite-rollout cloning (`train_six_pendulum_mjwarp_trajectory_bank_bc.py`, `export_six_pendulum_mjwarp_energy_teacher_trajectories.py`).
- PufferLib-oriented MuJoCo Warp environment and device-resident rollout with GPU score kernels (`six_pendulum_mjwarp_env.py`, `six_pendulum_mjwarp_device_rollout.py`, `six_pendulum_mjwarp_gpu_kernels.py`).
- iLQR teacher and frozen-target validation tooling for the model-based six-link reference (`solve_six_pendulum_mujoco_ilqr_teacher.py`, `validate_six_pendulum_frozen_target.py`, `export_six_pendulum_m1el_teacher_to_frozen_target.py`).

The full dated experiment log, with every negative result, is in `docs/ai-lab-six-pendulum-curriculum.md`. Local artifact paths in that log refer to files that are not included here.

## How to run

The scripts are written to run from the repository root. Dependencies are pinned inline in the original commands: Python 3.11, `mujoco-warp==3.9.0.1`, `torch==2.7.1`, `numpy==2.2.6`, `pufferlib`, `modal`. `uv` is the simplest way to get them.

Local (CPU, correctness checks only; the Mac build of MuJoCo Warp has no CUDA):

```bash
python3 scripts/generate-six-pendulum-mjcf.py

uv run --python 3.11 --with mujoco-warp==3.9.0.1 --with pufferlib --with numpy==2.2.6 \
  python scripts/six_pendulum_mjwarp_env.py --links 1 --nworld 4 --steps 128 --pose down

uv run --python 3.11 --with mujoco-warp==3.9.0.1 --with numpy==2.2.6 --with torch==2.7.1 \
  python scripts/train_six_pendulum_mjwarp_device_ppo.py --links 1 --nworld 8 \
  --rollout-steps 96 --eval-steps 480 --updates 4 --update-epochs 2 --pose down \
  --write-result out/device-ppo-train.json
```

Modal (GPU). Authenticate once with `modal setup`, then:

```bash
# one-link evolutionary solve, about 4 minutes on one L4
modal run --write-result out/pezzza-1link.json \
  scripts/modal-train-six-pendulum-pezzza-evolution.py \
  --smoke --control-hz 240 --population 1536 --generations 32 --profile curriculum

# MuJoCo PPO / SAC baselines
modal run scripts/modal-train-six-pendulum-mujoco-ppo.py --links 1 --smoke --curriculum down
modal run scripts/modal-train-six-pendulum-mujoco-sac.py --links 1 --smoke
```

`app/ailab/six-pendulum-cartpole/sixPendulumPolicy.json` is the exported one-link policy that the live page loads. No credentials are needed or stored in this repository; Modal reads its own token from the local Modal config.

## Hardware and cost

- Modal jobs used a single NVIDIA L4 (`gpu="L4"`, 2 hour timeout). The one-link solve took 229 s of wallclock.
- Local runs were on a Mac with CPU-only Warp, so throughput numbers from local runs are not meaningful. Roughly 721k simulated steps per second was recorded for the vectorized one-link trainer.
- Larger Modal runs for the PufferPPO lane were blocked by the workspace spend limit, which is why the reproduction of a multi-GPU, thousands-of-experiments sweep was never completed.

## Lessons learned

- Reward shaping decides discovery. Time above threshold plus a whip (near-top high angular speed) term and a loss-of-balance penalty was enough for evolutionary search. Plain PPO and SAC from the exact rest pose got a flat reward and learned nothing useful.
- Curriculum helps. Starting at low gravity and high friction, then moving to normal gravity, solved one link. Randomizing episode length only helped after whipping behavior already existed; applied to a policy that only reached sub-second near-catches it did not help (hold regressed to 0.01 s).
- Stabilization and swing-up are separate problems. Hold-start policies were solved repeatedly (including the 1M-parameter MinGRU) while exact down-start never was in the PPO lanes. Reset hardening, near-catch cloning, teacher anchoring and catch-gated rewards each failed to transfer to exact rest.
- Behavior cloning from one teacher trajectory transfers top stabilization but not the pump, catch, hold sequence.
- Do not force the chain straight during the pump phase for multi-link cases. The model-based six-link solution works because the chain keeps a controllable bend order until the catch.
- Simulator fidelity matters. A policy that solves one link in a custom simulator held for 0.0 s in MuJoCo Warp.
- Negative results are the bulk of the work here: lower-link PPO sweeps, SAC, TD3, CEM for two links, and BC and DAgger variants all stayed below the one-second gate.
